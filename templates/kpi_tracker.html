  • Units Launched comes from Ford's Launched Vans. Active Technicians and Offset Value are ours: edit them per month with "Edit techs & offset". A new month starts with the previous month's values.
  • Tracking (only while a month is in progress) = RO Count ÷ working days so far × working days in the month, rounded to 10. The day count comes from Ford's own ROs/Van/Day.
  • Commercial Mix = Ford Pro Count ÷ RO Count + 5 points (Ford's figure understates it).
  • Per Active Tech / Day columns divide by Active Technicians × working days so far. Revenue / Tech / Day uses Total RO Value (no offset).
  • Available = 138 × Units Launched for the full month. Eligible Offset Earned = RO Count ÷ Available. Offset Left On Table = (Available − RO Count) × Offset Value. Total Revenue = Total RO Value + Total Offset.
  • Full Year adds up every uploaded month of the year. Units, techs and offset value show the latest month, and Available and Tracking are summed month by month.
