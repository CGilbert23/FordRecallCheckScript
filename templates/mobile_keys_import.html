How this works

  • Every part on the invoice is read with its VIN, RO number and Net cost. List price is ignored.
  • Two parts on the same VIN and RO means a key with an insert — the cheaper one is recorded as the blank.
  • Keys are matched on VIN first, then RO. If the RO on the invoice was typed wrong, the match is still made on the VIN and flagged for you.
  • Nothing is saved until you look over the list and confirm it. Keys that already have part numbers are left unchecked so they can't be overwritten by accident.
  • Needs a real PDF from the parts system — a photo or a scan won't have text to read.
